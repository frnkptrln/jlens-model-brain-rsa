# Study A: whole-cortex searchlight summary

The full Study A implementation and reports are maintained in
[`lucas-nunn/natural-j-scenes`](https://github.com/lucas-nunn/natural-j-scenes).
For the paper, that analysis is pinned to commit
[`8389a3897a29c043bf12462b804400e999baa330`](https://github.com/lucas-nunn/natural-j-scenes/tree/8389a3897a29c043bf12462b804400e999baa330).

`layer_summary.csv` is the public group-level table used in Panel A of the paper
figure. `delta_j_minus_raw` is the Jacobian-transported minus raw RSA contrast;
the interval columns are the reported 95% confidence interval and `q_fdr` is the
reported FDR-adjusted value.
